Stock on the phone

You can now look up what is in stock from your iPhone, the same way you do on the console's Price check screen.

How the numbers get there

Inventory sheet
on the S: drive, never changed
→
Console
reads it when you export the phone bundle
→
Phone bundle
encrypted file, AirDropped
→
iPhone
Prices tab, Stock section

What you see

  • The same columns as the console: available, on order, back order, after PO, and notes such as "Reorder needed".
  • The search box and Group menu on the Prices tab filter stock too. A group shows the stock for the games in it.
  • If an item is linked to a stock code that is not on the inventory sheet, it says "Not on the inventory sheet" instead of a made-up zero.
  • A line shows when the sheet was saved and when the snapshot was made.

When it is old

Stock is from Oct 13, 2026. Re-export from the console for current numbers.

This appears when the snapshot is more than one day old. Export a new bundle from the console to refresh it.

What stays safe

  • Read-only. The phone cannot change stock, and nothing was added that writes to Azure or the inventory sheet.
  • Unit cost and vendor are never put in the bundle.
  • Older bundles still open. They simply say "No stock snapshot in this bundle."
